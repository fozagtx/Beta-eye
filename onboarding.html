Welcome to Beta-eye

Beta-eye simplifies page text locally in Chrome and keeps the original text restorable.

How Beta-eye Protects Pages

  • Simplification starts from your toolbar action.
  • Original text stays mapped in the page session.
  • Sensitive sites are skipped until you allow them.
  • Rules fallback works when Gemini Nano is unavailable.

Gemini Nano

Chrome controls Prompt API availability, model download, hardware checks, and policy checks. Beta-eye detects what is available and explains the current state in the popup.
