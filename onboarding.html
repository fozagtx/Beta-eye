Welcome to Redacto

Redacto finds confidential data locally and keeps the original text restorable.

How Redacto Protects Pages

  • Scanning starts from your toolbar action.
  • Original text stays mapped in the page session.
  • Sensitive sites are skipped until you allow them.
  • No model download or network connection is required.

Before sharing

Review the redacted result before pasting any text into an LLM. Redacto never uploads page text automatically.
